Who I am

I'm an IT Support student with a background in the arts, and a longtime interest in how technology can enhance creative expression. In the age of AI being pushed into everything and disenfranchising human artists, it's more important to me than ever to champion authentic, human-made works.

As I finish my last semester of college and continue developing both my technical and artistic skills, I want to document what inspires me across multiple axes - art, sports, and music. This site will be a place to document these inspirations that shape my personal work.
